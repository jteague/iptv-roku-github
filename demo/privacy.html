Teague Vision privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Short version: Teague Vision collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of mine for your data to go to. What you set up stays on your Roku, and the app only talks to the servers you tell it to use.

What I collect

Nothing. I (the developer) don't run any server that the app reports to. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it never reads your Roku's advertising ID or any other identifier meant for tracking. I don't know who uses the app, how often, or what they watch.

What stays on your Roku

To work, the app saves these in its own storage on your Roku:

  • The sources you add: their names, playlist and guide URLs, and any API key you enter
  • Your settings (guide size, refresh interval, what happens on launch)
  • Your favorites, hidden channels, reminders and the last channel you watched

None of this leaves your Roku except as described below. Removing the app from your Roku deletes it.

Who the app talks to

Only the servers you choose:

  • The playlist and guide URLs you enter
  • The stream servers listed in your playlist, when you watch a channel
  • The image hosts named in your playlist and guide, for channel logos and show posters
  • If you enter a Dispatcharr API key: your Dispatcharr server, to show and schedule recordings

Like any website, those servers see your IP address and what the app requests from them. What they do with that is up to them and their own privacy policies. I have no connection to them and no access to what they record.

If you use the demo playlist at jteague.github.io, GitHub serves the playlist and guide, and the streams come from NASA, Deutsche Welle, Akamai and Unified Streaming.

Roku itself

Your Roku device and Roku OS collect their own data under Roku's privacy policy. The app can't change or switch that off. The app does tell Roku OS when it has finished starting up, as Roku requires of every app. That signal carries no information about you or what you watch.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Checking my word

The source code is public at github.com/jteague/iptv-roku-github. Anyone can read it and confirm the above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Any change that collected data would also show up in the public source code.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
